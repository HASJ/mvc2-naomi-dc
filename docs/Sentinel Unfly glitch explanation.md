In Marvel vs. Capcom 2, the Sentinel "Unfly" bug (or Unfly Mode) is a glitch state that allows Sentinel to retain full mid-air control—including blocking and attacking—after deactivating flight.
Under normal game rules, turning off flight puts a character into a defenseless falling state until they touch the ground. Triggering Unfly Mode breaks this restriction, turning Sentinel into an offensive and defensive powerhouse.

How It Works Normally vs. Unfly Mode

• Normal Flight Deactivation: Activating flight counts as your single mid-air action. When you cancel flight (or run out of flight time), Sentinel drops straight down. During this fall, you cannot block, air-dash, or press any attack buttons until your feet hit the floor.
• Unfly Mode Active: The game forgets that you used up your mid-air action. When you cancel flight, Sentinel falls normally while keeping full access to air normals, specials, and air-blocking.

How to Trigger Unfly Mode

You cannot activate Unfly Mode on your own; the opponent has to trigger it for you.
• Getting Hit Out of the Air: If Sentinel is jumping or flying and gets hit by an attack that causes an air-reset (a flip recovery) or a soft knockdown (where he bounces and lands on his feet), the glitch activates.
• Persistent State: Once triggered, Sentinel stays in Unfly Mode even after landing. You can jump, super jump, and fly repeatedly while keeping the buff.

How Unfly Mode Ends

The glitched state remains active until one of a few specific conditions occurs:
• Normal Jump: Performing a standard jump and landing deactivates Unfly Mode. (Super jumping does not remove it, which is why high-level players rely heavily on super jumps.)
• Hard Knockdown: Getting hit by a sweep or a move that forces a hard knockdown resets Sentinel's state back to normal.
• Hit Limit: Each activation grants a limited number of "unfly hits" (roughly 14 to 15 hits), after which the state expires.

Why It Is So Strong

• Safe Air Approaches: Sentinel can fly in aggressively, cancel flight mid-air, and immediately block incoming anti-airs—eliminating his biggest vulnerability.
• Extended "Refly" Combos: Sentinel can hit an opponent in the air, unfly, hit them again on the way down, and immediately re-enter flight to continue the combo. This enables massive, high-damage corner loops and resets.
• Instant High/Low Mixups: Canceling flight close to the ground lets Sentinel drop with fast overhead attacks or land immediately into low attacks.
